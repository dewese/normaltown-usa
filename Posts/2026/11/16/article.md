# Your Seed Phrase Is the Master Key. Treat It That Way

> A seed phrase is a list of 12 to 24 ordinary words that can rebuild your bitcoin wallet from scratch. If your device breaks or gets lost, those words bring your bitcoin back. If anyone else gets them, they can take everything. Write it down on paper, keep it somewhere safe and offline, and never type it into a website or tell it to anyone who asks.

If you ever move bitcoin to a wallet you control yourself, the setup will show you a list of words and tell you to write them down. That list is the single most important thing in the whole process. Here's what it is and how to treat it.

## The short answer

A seed phrase, also called a recovery phrase, is a list of 12 to 24 common words in a specific order. Something like "river, table, orange, candle" and so on, though yours will be random.

Those words hold all the information needed to rebuild your wallet. Remember from [what a bitcoin wallet actually is](/p/what-is-a-bitcoin-wallet/) that a wallet doesn't hold coins. It holds keys, the secret codes that prove which bitcoin on the public record belongs to you. The seed phrase is the source those keys are made from. Have the words, and you have the keys.

## The key code for your truck

Most cars and trucks have a key code. It's a short number from the factory. If you lose every key you own, a dealer can use that code to cut you a brand new one that starts the truck.

Now imagine a stranger found that code. They could cut their own key, walk out to your driveway, and drive off. Nothing about the truck would look different until it was gone.

A seed phrase is the key code for your bitcoin. Lose your hardware wallet in a flood, and the words let you rebuild it on a new device, even one from a different brand. Let a stranger see the words, and they can rebuild your wallet on their device and empty it.

## Why it matters so much

With a wallet you control yourself, there's no customer service. No "forgot password" button. No bank to call. That's the trade for being in full control, which I explained in [not your keys, what it means](/p/not-your-keys-what-it-means/).

The seed phrase is your only backup. River Learn's lesson cites an estimate that around 20% of all bitcoin is considered lost, often because people lost access to their wallets. That's a lot of bitcoin nobody can ever move again.

## How to treat it

These are the basics River Learn and most wallet makers agree on:

- **Write it on paper.** Use the card that came with your device, or a notebook.
- **Never store it online.** No photos, no notes app, no email to yourself, no cloud drive. Anything online can be found by someone else.
- **Keep it somewhere safe.** Somewhere protected from fire, water, and curious visitors.
- **Never give it to anyone.** No real company will ever ask for your seed phrase. Not River, not a wallet maker, not "support." Anyone who asks is trying to rob you.

That last one is where most theft happens. People don't get hacked so much as tricked. I'll cover that in [bitcoin scams and the lock I use](/p/bitcoin-scams-and-forcefield/).

## The honest part

Holding your own keys moves the risk from a company to you. If you lose the device and the words, the bitcoin is gone for good. If someone else gets the words, same result. Be honest with yourself about whether you're ready for that job.

And none of this protects you from the price. Bitcoin swings hard and has stayed down for years at a time. Only use money you can leave alone.

## Questions I get about this

### What is a seed phrase in bitcoin?

A list of 12 to 24 words, in order, that can rebuild a bitcoin wallet. It's the master backup for a wallet you control yourself.

### Can I store my seed phrase on my phone?

Most guidance, including River Learn's, says no. Keep it offline, on paper or another physical backup. Anything stored online or on a connected device can be found by someone else.

### What happens if I lose my seed phrase?

If you still have your working wallet, you can usually move your bitcoin to a new wallet with a new phrase and write that one down. If you lose both the device and the words, the bitcoin can't be recovered.
