# Why the CrowdHealth Bill Climbs Toward Year End

> CrowdHealth's average monthly cost tends to rise in the fall. For a single member under 55 it went from $135 in July 2026 to $165 in October, counting the $60 fee. The last two times, it dropped back in January. That's a pattern, not a promise. The number that protects you is the cap: under 55, the crowd can never ask more than $140 a month, so your worst month is $200.

If you're a CrowdHealth member, you've probably noticed the monthly number creeping up lately. You're not imagining it. And CrowdHealth says so too: the ask ticks up toward year end, same as past years.

Here are the real numbers, what they mean, and how to plan for them.

So far in 2026, the range has been $125 to $165. October was the high.

For ages 55 to 64, it went from $190 in January to $270 in October. For a family of four, it went from $435 in January to $555 in October.

## The January drop

Here's the part I find interesting. Look at the last two Januaries.

At the end of 2024, a member under 55 paid an average of $165 in December. In January 2025, it was $140.

At the end of 2025, it was $145 in December. In January 2026, it was $125.

Even better, CrowdHealth's own table shows the fee went up each of those Januaries too. Subtract the average ask from the total and you get the fee: $50 in late 2024, $55 in 2025, $60 in 2026. The crowd ask fell by more than the fee rose, so the total still dropped.

## Why it climbs

CrowdHealth doesn't give a reason on its cost page, so I won't make one up. What I can tell you is that it's not random. It happened in both years on the chart.

Think of it like the electric bill in a Texas August. You don't know the exact number in advance. But you know it's coming, you know roughly how high it goes, and you don't panic when it shows up.

## How I plan for it

**Budget for the cap, not the average.** Under 55, the crowd can ask up to $140 a month. With the $60 fee, that's $200. From 55 to 64, it's $340. For a family of four or more, it's $660. If your budget handles the cap, a high month is just a month. I walk through this in [how much CrowdHealth costs](/p/crowdhealth-cost/).

**Remember the fee goes up in January.** Starting January 1, 2027, the advocacy fee is $65 per member, not $60. If the crowd caps stay the same, the worst month becomes $205 under 55, $345 from 55 to 64, and $680 for a family of four. So even if the ask drops in January like it has before, part of that drop goes to the higher fee. The other 2027 changes are in [what changes at CrowdHealth on January 1, 2027](/p/crowdhealth-2027-changes/).

**Look at the year, not the month.** A $165 October next to a $125 January averages out. Compare your yearly total to insurance, which I do in [the all-in yearly cost, side by side](/p/the-all-in-yearly-cost-side-by-side/).

## The honest part

Two Januaries is not a law of nature. The ask could stay high. It could go higher. The crowd ask is a share of real members' bills, and nobody knows next month's bills in advance.

That's the trade. Insurance gives you a fixed monthly bill and a big deductible. CrowdHealth gives you a monthly number that moves, with a hard ceiling. If a number that moves would keep you up at night, even with a ceiling, that matters. [Who health sharing is wrong for](/p/who-health-sharing-is-wrong-for/) covers it.

## Questions I get about this

### Is the October 2026 number the highest it's been?

For a single member under 55, $165 matches December 2024 as the highest month on CrowdHealth's chart. It's still $35 under the $200 cap.

### Will it drop in January 2027?

Nobody knows. It dropped the last two Januaries. The fee also goes up $5 per member on January 1, 2027, so the total may not drop as much even if the ask does.

### Does the Longevity Discount help with this?

It can. CrowdHealth's new [Longevity Discount](/p/crowdhealth-longevity-discount/) takes up to 20% off the crowd contribution in 2027 for passing certain health tests. It's hard to qualify for, so don't count on it.
