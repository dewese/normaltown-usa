# Don't Trust, Check: How to See That River Really Holds the Bitcoin

> A proof of reserves is a company showing, in public, that it holds at least as much bitcoin as it owes its customers. River publishes one every month: the bitcoin it holds, which anyone can see on bitcoin's public record, next to an anonymized list of every customer balance. You can look up your own account on that list. It's a snapshot, not a guarantee, so River's financial statements matter too.

When you leave bitcoin with a company, you're trusting them to actually have it. Some companies in the past didn't, and their customers found out the hard way. A proof of reserves is a way to check instead of just trust. Here's how River's works.

## The short answer

As of October 2026, River publishes a proof of reserves every month. It has two halves:

- **Assets:** the bitcoin River holds in its cold storage. Cold storage just means wallets kept offline. Because bitcoin runs on a public record, anyone can look those wallets up on a free website called a block explorer, like mempool.space.
- **Liabilities:** what River owes customers. River publishes an anonymized list of every client's bitcoin balance. "Anonymized" means no names, just account IDs and amounts.

If assets are bigger than liabilities, River holds more bitcoin than it owes. That comparison is called the reserve ratio, and over 100% is what you want to see.

## The club treasurer

Picture the treasurer of a neighborhood club. Every member has paid dues, and the treasurer says the money's all in the bank.

A treasurer you can trust doesn't just say "it's fine." They hold up the bank statement for everyone to see. Then they post a list of every member's balance, with ID numbers instead of names. You find your number, check that your amount is right, and compare the bank total to the list total.

That's a proof of reserves. The bank statement is the bitcoin anyone can see on the public record. The list is the liabilities. And you are the member checking your own line.

## What River's October proof showed

River's proof dated October 6, 2026 listed:

- **Assets:** 33,404.73 bitcoin
- **Liabilities:** 33,332.42 bitcoin
- **Reserve ratio:** above 100%

As of October 2026, River's monthly history going back to November 2025 shows a ratio above 100% every month.

River also has to prove it actually controls those wallets, not just that the wallets exist. It does that by sending a small transaction for an amount set ahead of time. Only someone holding the keys could do that.

## How to check your own balance

Find your River account ID in your account. Then search for it on River's published list of liabilities. If your balance shows up with the right amount, you know you were counted. That's the part that makes this different from a company just posting a big number.

## Why the financials page matters too

River Learn's own lesson is upfront about this, and I appreciate that. A proof of reserves is a snapshot. It shows one moment in time. It also only covers bitcoin. It doesn't show every debt a company has, like loans or bills it owes to other businesses.

That's why River also publishes a company financials page. As of October 2026, River says it's profitable with zero debt, and that it had a $68.1 million corporate treasury at the end of 2025. It says an independent accounting firm audits it every year, and you can download its 2025 statements. River also says it doesn't lend out client bitcoin.

The two pages work together. Reserves say "we hold your bitcoin." Financials say "and we're not drowning somewhere else."

A proof of reserves lowers your risk. It doesn't erase it. It's a monthly snapshot, River is still a company with its own risks, and a company could have problems between snapshots.

## Questions I get about this

### What is a proof of reserves?

It's a public check that a company holds at least as much of an asset as it owes customers. For bitcoin, the company shows its wallets on bitcoin's public record and publishes a list of customer balances, so anyone can compare.

### How often does River publish proof of reserves?

Monthly, as of October 2026. Its proof dated October 6, 2026 showed 33,404.73 bitcoin in assets against 33,332.42 bitcoin in customer balances.

### Does a proof of reserves mean my bitcoin is 100% safe?

No. It's a snapshot in time and it doesn't show every debt a company has. It's a good sign, and pairing it with audited financials is better, but holding your own keys is the only way to take the company out of the picture.
