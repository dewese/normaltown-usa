# CrowdHealth's Longevity Discount: Up to 20% Off for Three Blood and Body Tests

> CrowdHealth's new Longevity Discount Program takes up to 20% off your monthly crowdfunding contribution in 2027 if you pass health tests. There are three: fasting insulin, a blood test for inflammation called hs-CRP, and a DEXA scan for belly fat. Pass one and you get 10%. Pass two and you get 20%. Tests must be done and sent in between October 1, 2026 and January 31, 2027. If two adults are on the account, both have to pass, or nobody gets it.

Most health plans charge you the same whether you run marathons or live on the couch. CrowdHealth just launched something different. Get healthier on paper, and your monthly cost goes down.

Here's how it works, and who should skip it.

## How the discount works

The discount comes off your monthly contribution, the part that goes to other members' bills. CrowdHealth's page describes it as a discount on your monthly crowdfunding ask. It doesn't mention the flat advocacy fee, so I'd assume that part stays the same.

There are three tests to choose from:

| Test | What you need to pass | Crowdfunded up to |
|---|---|---|
| Fasting insulin | Below 5 µIU/mL | $25 |
| hs-CRP (inflammation) | Below 1 mg/L | $25 |
| DEXA scan, visceral fat | Very low visceral fat (see below) | $100 |

Visceral fat is the fat packed around your organs, not the kind you can pinch. A DEXA scan is a low-dose body scan that measures it. CrowdHealth's page states the DEXA bar two ways: 1 lb or less of visceral fat, and bottom quarter for your age and sex. Check the current wording before you book. If your scan report doesn't include visceral fat, it won't count.

Pass one test and you get 10% off. Pass two and you get 20% off. CrowdHealth will crowdfund up to two tests per eligible adult, at the amounts above.

## The fine print that matters

**Both adults have to pass.** If you and a spouse are on the account, each of you has to take part and pass at least one test for 10%, and two tests each for 20%. If only one of you passes, the account gets nothing.

**The window is short.** Tests have to be done and turned in through the app between October 1, 2026 and January 31, 2027. Old results don't count. The discount starts in January 2027, or February if you test in January.

**It's one year at a time.** The discount only covers the next calendar year. To keep it, you test again every year.

**It's for the Original membership only.** Black Swan members, the ones with the $15,000 commitment, can't use it.

**It doesn't stack with a referral code.** If you join with a promo or referral code, including mine, the Longevity Discount waits until that code runs out, then kicks in.

There's also a Q4 2026 special running alongside it: any two of these tests can be crowdfunded if you do them and turn them in this quarter. CrowdHealth won't fund the same test twice across the two programs.

## What 20% looks like

Think of it like a safe-driver discount on car coverage. You don't get paid to drive well. You just pay a little less because you're less likely to cost the group money.

CrowdHealth's October 2026 average ask for a single member under 55 was $105, on top of the $60 fee. Twenty percent of $105 is $21 a month. Over a year, that's $252, roughly.

For a family, the crowd part is bigger, so the dollars are bigger. CrowdHealth's October average for a family of four was $315 in crowd ask. Twenty percent of that would be $63 a month. Those are averages, not promises. Your real ask moves every month.

## The honest part

CrowdHealth says it straight: these numbers are tough on purpose. A fasting insulin below 5 is a hard bar for a lot of healthy-looking adults. Most people won't pass all three, and plenty won't pass any.

So don't join CrowdHealth because of this discount. Join because the rest of it fits your family. Treat the discount as a bonus that might show up later.

And if you have an ongoing condition, this program isn't the thing to focus on. Start with [who health sharing is wrong for](/p/who-health-sharing-is-wrong-for/).

The part I like is the idea behind it. Insurance companies don't get paid to keep you well, which I wrote about in [nobody gets paid to make you well](/p/nobody-gets-paid-to-make-you-well/). This is a small push the other way.

## Questions I get about this

### Do I have to do all three tests?

No. One passed test is enough for 10%. Two passed tests get you 20%. The DEXA scan is the one CrowdHealth crowdfunds the most for, up to $100. If two adults are on the account, the 10% and 20% levels apply per adult.

### Can I retake a test if I fail?

Yes. But CrowdHealth only crowdfunds up to two tests per eligible adult per year. Any extra tests are on you.

### Does my teenager have to pass too?

No. Only the primary account holder and a spouse, if there is one, take part. Dependents don't test.
