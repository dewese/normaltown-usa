# The CrowdHealth Year-End Checklist

> Before December 31: submit any bills that are getting close to the 6-month limit, use your 2026 wellness amount (one service, up to $300), and book your Longevity Discount tests if you want the discount to start in January. Open a health event before any care you've scheduled for January. Then budget for the advocacy fee going from $60 to $65 per member starting January 1, 2027, and read the other 2027 changes.

December gets busy. My family of four has been on CrowdHealth since 2022, after we canceled an expensive insurance plan, and here's my short list of CrowdHealth things worth doing before the calendar flips. Most take ten minutes in the app.

## 1. Submit your old bills

CrowdHealth's member guide says bills must be submitted within 6 months of the date of service, barring extenuating circumstances. So anything from early summer is close to the line. A visit on June 1 runs out around December 1. A visit on July 15 runs out around January 15.

Go through the drawer, the email inbox, and the patient portals. Scan anything that belongs to a health event into the app. Going forward, the easy habit is to submit each bill as soon as it arrives. If you're not sure it'll pass $500, submit it anyway so it's on file if follow-up care pushes it over. The paperwork side is in [the paperwork is real](/p/the-paperwork-is-real/).

## 2. Use your 2026 wellness amount

On the Original membership, each member gets up to $300 for an annual wellness event, with the $500 commitment waived. Two catches for 2026:

- **It's one service.** In 2026, the guide allows one preventive or screening exam or test. Pick the one that matters most.
- **It has to happen by December 31** to count for 2026.

Starting January 1, 2027, wellness gets more flexible: several preventive services can share the same $300. Kids three and up have their own wellness event. Black Swan doesn't include wellness at all. One member's experience with routine visits is in [this story about six routine visits](/p/six-routine-visits-one-covered/).

Think of it like a gift card that expires December 31. Nobody's going to remind you, and an unused one is just a piece of plastic.

## 3. Book your Longevity Discount tests

The Longevity Discount can take up to 20% off your monthly contribution, based on how many approved tests you complete and pass. There are three tests: fasting insulin, hs-CRP, and a DEXA scan for visceral fat. The primary account holder and their spouse each need to complete **at least one** of them between October 1, 2026 and January 31, 2027, and submit the results and receipts in the app by January 31.

The tests themselves are eligible for crowdfunding, up to 2 per adult. Timing matters: test by December and the discount starts in January 2027. Test in January and it starts in February. So December 31 isn't the hard deadline here, but it's the one that gets you an extra month of savings. The full rules are in [CrowdHealth's Longevity Discount](/p/crowdhealth-longevity-discount/).

## 4. Open a health event before scheduled care

Got a surgery, scan, or specialist visit on the calendar for January? Open the health event and call your Care Advocate now. The member guide already asks for this before scheduled care, and the 2027 guide makes it even clearer: open a health event before scheduled or potentially expensive care. Skipping it can hurt your funding. The tools for this are in [the member tools that save money before a bill](/p/crowdhealth-member-tools/).

## 5. Budget for the $65 fee

Starting January 1, 2027, the advocacy fee goes from $60 to $65 per member. For my family of four, that's $260 a month instead of $240, or $240 more a year. If the crowd caps stay the same, the worst case for a single member under 55 becomes $205 a month.

Also expect the monthly ask to stay high through December. CrowdHealth's own history shows it climbing late in the year and dropping back each January so far. That's a pattern, not a promise. I walk through it in [why the bill climbs toward year end](/p/why-the-crowdhealth-bill-climbs-at-year-end/).

## 6. Read the 2027 changes

The short version, all starting January 1, 2027:

- Virtual visits from any licensed provider, crowdfunded up to $129 each without the $500.
- Talk therapy moves under the $300 wellness amount.
- PT, OT, speech, pelvic floor, and chiropractic limited to 15 visits per health event, with more only after a clinical review.
- Type 2 diabetes care moves to the Exit Diabetes product.

My full rundown is in [what changes at CrowdHealth on January 1, 2027](/p/crowdhealth-2027-changes/).

## The honest part

None of this is hard, but none of it happens on its own. CrowdHealth isn't insurance, and nobody is going to file your bills or book your wellness visit for you. Your Care Advocate will explain the steps, but the submitting, the calls, and paying your providers are on you. The 6-month rule in particular is a real deadline. Miss it, and a bill you could have submitted is just yours.

And if you're shopping, not renewing, the [open enrollment post](/p/open-enrollment-before-you-auto-renew/) has the dates that matter.

## Questions I get about this

### Does the wellness amount roll over?

No. The 2026 wellness amount has to be used by December 31, 2026. Then the 2027 version starts fresh.

### What if I'm in the middle of physical therapy when the year changes?

Starting January 1, 2027, PT and similar therapy is limited to 15 visits per health event unless a clinical review approves more, and it has to treat a specific problem. Ask your Care Advocate how that applies to an event that's already open.

### Do I need to do anything for the fee change?

No action needed. It's $5 more per member per month, starting January 1, 2027. Just update your budget.
