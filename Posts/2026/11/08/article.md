# What Changes at CrowdHealth on January 1, 2027

> Starting January 1, 2027, CrowdHealth's monthly fee goes from $60 to $65 per member. Annual wellness can cover several preventive services up to $300. Talk therapy moves under that same $300. Virtual visits can be with any licensed provider, up to $129 each, without paying your $500 first. Physical therapy and similar care is limited to 15 visits per health event unless a review approves more. Type 2 diabetes care moves to a separate product.

Every year CrowdHealth updates its member guide, the rulebook for what the crowd pays for. The 2027 version is out, and it starts January 1. Most of it is cleanup. A few parts change what you pay or what gets funded.

Here's the whole list in plain English, with the parts that help you and the parts that don't.

## The fee goes up $5

Starting January 1, 2027, the monthly advocacy fee goes from $60 to $65 per member. That's the flat part that pays CrowdHealth for the app, the bill negotiators, and your Care Advocate.

For a family of four, that's $260 a month in fees instead of $240. Until January 1, the $60 is still the number.

If the monthly crowd caps stay where they are now, the worst possible month becomes $205 for a member under 55, $345 from 55 to 64, and $680 for a family of four. CrowdHealth hasn't said the caps are changing, but I'll update this if it does.

## What gets better

**Annual wellness gets flexible.** Today you get one preventive service a year, crowdfunded up to $300 without paying your $500 first. Starting in 2027, you can split that $300 across several preventive services through the year. A checkup in March and lab work in October, for example.

**Virtual care opens up.** Today the funded virtual visits run through HealthTap in the app. Starting in 2027, virtual primary care and urgent care can come from any licensed provider. Each visit can be crowdfunded up to $129 without meeting the $500 first. HealthTap stays in the app too.

## What gets tighter

**Talk therapy moves under wellness.** Today virtual talk therapy is its own funded health event. Starting January 1, it isn't. Therapy can go through your annual wellness instead, but it shares that same $300 with your checkups. Discounted therapy options are still available through the member tools.

**Physical therapy and similar care gets a limit.** This covers physical, occupational, speech, and pelvic floor therapy, plus chiropractic care. It has to treat a specific problem tied to a health event. Maintenance care isn't eligible. And it's limited to 15 visits per health event. More than 15 isn't an automatic no, but it takes a clinical review.

**Type 2 diabetes care leaves the main product.** Starting January 1, it isn't crowdfunded on the Original or Black Swan memberships. Members with Type 2 diabetes who want help from the crowd have to move to CrowdHealth's Exit Diabetes product and join a reversal program.

**Long testing needs a plan.** If you've been getting tests for 12 months without a diagnosis, CrowdHealth may ask for a clinical review before funding more.

## The small stuff

- **Open a health event first.** The guide now says it plainly: tell CrowdHealth before scheduled or potentially expensive care. I'd do that anyway. It's how you get price help before the bill.
- **Cataract surgery** fair prices are based on a standard lens. Fancy lens upgrades don't set the price.
- **Be decent.** Abusive behavior toward staff, doctors, or other members can end your membership.
- **Black Swan** members, the ones with the $15,000 commitment, don't get the wellness, virtual care, or therapy pieces.

## What I make of it

Think of it like the rules at a community pool. Every year the board posts a new sign by the gate. Most lines are the same. A couple of new ones change how you swim.

For a family that mostly uses checkups and the odd video visit, the wellness and virtual care changes are a small win, and the $5 fee is a small cost. The tighter rules mostly hit people who use a lot of therapy, chiropractic, or ongoing diabetes care.

## The honest part

If you rely on weekly chiropractic or talk therapy, 2027 is less generous than 2026. Do the math before you renew your plans. If you have Type 2 diabetes, CrowdHealth's main product is no longer the place for that care, and [who health sharing is wrong for](/p/who-health-sharing-is-wrong-for/) applies to you.

## Questions I get about this

### Does the $65 apply to kids?

Yes. The fee is per member, children included. A family of four goes from $240 to $260 a month in fees.

### Will the $99 intro price change?

CrowdHealth hasn't announced a change. Right now, new members who join with a referral code get their first 3 months at $99 a month per member.

### Do I need to do anything on January 1?

Not that I can see. The update page doesn't ask members to opt in; the new rules simply take effect. If you're in the middle of physical therapy, ask your Care Advocate how the 15-visit limit applies to your event.
