# CrowdHealth's Bitcoin Savings Program: Your Unused Crowdfunding, Swept Into Bitcoin

> CrowdHealth partners with Fold, a separate bitcoin company. If you link a Fold+ account, each month the gap between your crowdfunding maximum and what you were actually asked to give gets swept into it. In October 2026 that gap was about $35 for a single member under 55. It's your own money, not a bonus, and bitcoin's price swings hard.

Every month CrowdHealth asks members to chip in toward other members' bills. That ask has a ceiling. Most months the crowd needs less than the ceiling. So where does the difference go?

Normally, nowhere. It just stays in your checking account. With this program, it gets moved into bitcoin savings instead.

## How does the sweep work?

CrowdHealth's program page lays it out in four steps:

1. **Link a Fold+ account.** You give CrowdHealth your Fold+ account email, account number, and routing number.
2. **Give as usual.** Your monthly crowdfunding contributions happen the same way they always do.
3. **The gap gets swept.** CrowdHealth takes the difference between your crowdfunding maximum and what you actually contributed, and transfers it to your Fold+ account automatically.
4. **The subscription is covered.** CrowdHealth pays for your Fold+ subscription for as long as you're a member.

Quick note on names. Fold is its own company. CrowdHealth partners with it, but they're two separate businesses with two separate apps and two separate sets of rules.

## What does that look like in real numbers?

Here are the monthly crowdfunding maximums from CrowdHealth's program page:

- **Original, ages 0 to 54:** $140
- **Original, ages 55 to 64:** $280
- **Original, families of four or more:** $420
- **Black Swan:** $25, $50, and $75 for the same groups, per CrowdHealth's Black Swan member guide

Now the real month. CrowdHealth posted an October 2026 total of $165 for a single member under 55. Take away the $60 advocacy fee and the average ask was $105.

So: $140 maximum, minus $105 asked, is about **$35** swept into Fold+ that month.

For a family of four, October's posted total was $555. Take away $240 in fees (four people at $60) and the ask was $315. Against a $420 household ceiling, that's about **$105**.

The gap moves every month. Earlier in 2026, when the under-55 total was $125, the ask was $65 and the gap would have been about $75. The ask tends to climb late in the year, so the sweep shrinks right when the crowd needs more. I explain that pattern in [why the bill climbs toward year end](/p/why-the-crowdhealth-bill-climbs-at-year-end/). And these are averages. Your own ask can be a little higher or lower.

## Think of it like a round-up app

You know those apps that round your $4.30 coffee up to $5 and save the 70 cents? This is the same idea, except the round-up goes all the way to your ceiling.

You budget the maximum every month. Whatever the crowd didn't need goes to savings instead of sitting in checking.

## The honest part

**It's your money, not a gift.** Nobody is handing you free bitcoin. The swept amount is money that would otherwise have stayed in your account. What this really does is set your health budget at the maximum: $200 a month for a single member under 55 with today's $60 fee, or $205 starting January 1, 2027, when the fee goes to $65 (if the crowd caps stay the same). If paying the max is a stretch, this makes your month tighter, not looser.

**Bitcoin's price swings hard.** It can drop a lot and stay down for years. The $35 swept in October could be worth less by Christmas. Only sweep money you can leave alone, and keep your $500 member commitment in plain cash. I wrote about that in [why my $500 cushion lives in cash, not bitcoin](/p/why-my-500-cushion-lives-in-cash/).

**It's moving to a separate company.** Once the money lands in Fold+, Fold's app, Fold's terms, and Fold's fees apply. CrowdHealth's page calls it bitcoin savings, but it doesn't spell out how and when each swept dollar gets converted, or how withdrawals work. Read Fold's terms and ask your Care Advocate before you link an account.

## Questions I get about this

### Does the Fold+ program cost extra?

CrowdHealth says it covers your Fold+ subscription while you're a member. The real cost is that you're sending the full maximum out the door every month. Any fees inside Fold for buying or selling are Fold's, so check their terms.

### Does it work on Black Swan?

Black Swan's monthly request is much smaller ($25 for a member under 55, per its member guide), so the sweep would be based on that smaller number and the gap would be smaller. Confirm with CrowdHealth before you count on it.

### Is it a good idea?

If you can comfortably budget the maximum and you already want to save some money in bitcoin, it automates the habit. If the maximum would pinch, skip it. Your health budget comes first.
