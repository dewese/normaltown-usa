# Zero-Fee Recurring Buys: Bitcoin Savings on Autopilot

> A recurring buy is an automatic purchase of the same dollar amount of bitcoin on a schedule you pick, like every week. As of October 2026, River charges no fee on recurring buys after the first 7 days, while a one-time buy costs 1.00%. It's how I buy bitcoin: set it once, then leave it alone.

This is the exact setup I use. Every week, River buys the same dollar amount of bitcoin for me automatically. I don't pick the day. I don't check the price first. It just happens, and as of October 2026, it happens without a fee.

## What a recurring buy is

A recurring buy is a standing order. You tell River three things: how much, how often, and where the money comes from. Then it repeats until you cancel it.

As of October 2026, River lets you pick hourly, daily, weekly, every two weeks, the 1st and 15th of the month, or monthly. The money can come from a linked bank account or from cash you keep in your River account.

You can change the amount or the payment method any time. If you want a different schedule, you cancel the old one and start a new one.

## Where the zero fee comes in

As of October 2026, a regular one-time buy on River costs 1.00% (on amounts up to $1 million). Recurring buys cost 0% after the first 7 days.

Here's how River explains the first week. On a weekly, every-two-weeks, 1st and 15th, or monthly schedule, your very first order pays the normal fee and every order after that is free. On a daily or hourly schedule, buys are free starting on day 8.

Let's do the math on a $25 weekly buy, using the October 2026 numbers:

- **One-time buys, every week by hand:** 1.00% of $25 is 25 cents. Fifty-two weeks of that is $13 a year in fees.
- **A recurring buy:** the first week pays 25 cents. The other 51 weeks pay nothing.

Thirteen dollars isn't life-changing. But it's thirteen dollars of bitcoin you keep instead of handing over, every year, for doing the lazy thing.

One fine-print item worth knowing. River says its quoted prices include a spread, which is a small markup built into the price itself. As of October 2026, River says that's typically about 0.25% on buys. So "zero fee" means no fee line on top. It doesn't mean the price has no markup at all.

## The sprinkler timer

Think about watering a lawn. You could stand out there with a hose every evening, deciding whether today looks dry enough. Or you could set the sprinkler timer once and go inside.

The timer doesn't care if it rained yesterday. It doesn't get nervous. It just waters on schedule, and over a summer the lawn gets what it needs.

A recurring buy is a sprinkler timer for your savings. It takes your mood out of it. That matters more than people think, because the moment you start deciding each week, you start guessing. I explained why I quit guessing in [why I don't try to time the price](/p/why-i-dont-try-to-time-the-price/).

Buying the same amount on a schedule has a name, dollar-cost averaging. I wrote the plain version in [a little each week beats betting it all](/p/a-little-each-week-beats-betting-it-all/), and River Learn has a lesson on it too.

## One detail on daily buys

If you choose daily or hourly, River doesn't pull money from your bank every single day. As of October 2026, it pulls once a week to cover the week ahead. So a $10 daily buy takes $70 from your bank each week. Make sure that money is sitting there.

## The honest part

Automatic doesn't mean safe. A recurring buy keeps buying when the price is falling, and bitcoin has fallen hard before and stayed down for years. A steady schedule protects you from bad timing. It doesn't protect you from the price going down.

So pick an amount you'd never miss. If losing it would change your week, it's too big. And keep your [emergency fund](/p/the-first-1000-emergency-fund/) in plain cash first.

River is also a company, with its own risks, and it changes its fees and products from time to time. The numbers here are as of October 2026.

## Questions I get about this

### Is River's recurring buy really free?

As of October 2026, River charges a 0% fee on recurring buys after the first 7 days. On weekly, every-two-weeks, 1st and 15th, or monthly schedules, the first order pays the normal fee. River's prices include a small spread, so check the quoted price when you set it up.

### How often should my recurring buy run?

There's no magic answer. Weekly is simple and it's what I do. The schedule matters less than picking an amount you can keep going without stress.

### Can I stop a recurring buy?

Yes. It runs until you cancel it, and you can cancel any time. You can also change the amount without starting over.
