# Black Swan: CrowdHealth's $15,000 Version, and Who It's For

> Black Swan is CrowdHealth's second product. You pay the first $15,000 of each health event yourself, and only the bills above that go to the crowd. In exchange, the monthly crowdfunding request is much lower: $25 for a member under 55, against up to $140 on the Original plan. There's no wellness, maternity, funded virtual care, talk therapy, or quarterly specials. It fits people with a big cash cushion and no baby plans.

Most of what I write about CrowdHealth is the Original plan, the one with the $500 member commitment. But when you open the pricing calculator, there's a second button that says $15,000. That's Black Swan.

The name comes from the idea of a "black swan" event: rare, huge, and hard to see coming. That's the only thing this version is built for.

## How is Black Swan different?

On Original, you pay the first $500 of each health event and the crowd is asked to fund eligible bills above that. On Black Swan, that number is **$15,000**.

CrowdHealth's own Black Swan guide gives examples. A hernia surgery at about $3,500 or a knee arthroscopy at about $7,500 wouldn't go to the crowd at all. You'd pay those yourself. A $30,000 back surgery would: you pay $15,000, the crowd is asked for the rest.

You still get the parts that help with the price. The guide lists a Care Advocate, cash-pay rates, discount prescriptions, and bill negotiation.

## What do you give up?

According to CrowdHealth's member tools page and the Black Swan guide, these aren't part of Black Swan:

- **Annual wellness.** Checkups and preventive care are on you. So are children's well visits and vaccines.
- **Maternity.** Pregnancy isn't eligible. CrowdHealth says to move to Original before conception, and you need to be on Original at least 300 days before the due date.
- **Funded virtual care.** You can still book a virtual visit in the app, but you pay for it and it isn't crowdfunded.
- **Virtual talk therapy and quarterly specials.** Not available.

One more that matters for families: if several of you are hurt in the same car accident, each person opens a separate health event with a separate $15,000 commitment.

## Think of it like a storm cellar

A storm cellar doesn't help when it rains. It doesn't help with hail, either. It's there for the tornado. Every ordinary storm, you handle on your own.

That's Black Swan. Every ordinary bill is yours. The crowd is for the tornado.

## The honest part

Do the math both ways. At the ceilings, Black Swan saves a single member under 55 about $115 a month, or about $1,380 a year. The difference in what you pay per event is $14,500. One mid-sized surgery could eat about 10 years of those savings.

So it's a fit if:

- You have well over $15,000 in savings you can reach quickly, and more for a family, since each person's event is separate.
- You're healthy, rarely see a doctor, and are fine paying cash for the small stuff.
- You aren't planning a pregnancy.

It's a poor fit if a $15,000 bill would sink you, if you'd use the wellness and virtual care perks, or if a baby might be in your future. And like Original, it's healthcare crowdfunding, not insurance. There's no legal promise a bill gets funded.

## Questions I get about this

### Can I switch between Black Swan and Original?

Yes, once a year. But a health event follows the product you were on when the symptoms started or the injury happened. Switch to Original after your back starts hurting, and that back problem still follows Black Swan's rules.

### Is Black Swan the cheapest way to use CrowdHealth?

Month to month, yes. Over time, only if you never have a big event. If you're comparing it with a $500 commitment, read [the $500 you pay vs the deductible you don't](/p/the-500-you-pay-vs-the-deductible-you-dont/) and [what CrowdHealth costs](/p/crowdhealth-cost/).
