> "Crypto" is the catch-all word for thousands of digital coins. Bitcoin was the first, and it has a fixed supply that no company or person controls. When people find out I own bitcoin, they usually ask what "crypto" I'm in. The honest answer: almost none. I do own a little Solana, about 1% of what I hold, mostly out of curiosity. Bitcoin is the one that matters to me. Here's the difference, and why I keep it simple.

## The short answer

"Crypto" is short for cryptocurrency. It's a big umbrella over thousands of digital coins and tokens. Bitcoin is one of them. It was the first, and it's still the biggest.

The thing that sets bitcoin apart for me isn't the technology. It's who's in charge. Nobody is.

Bitcoin has a hard limit of 21 million coins. That limit is written into the rules that thousands of computers around the world all follow. No company owns it. No CEO can decide to make more. Changing the rules would take agreement from a huge, scattered crowd of people who mostly want the rules left alone.

Most other coins don't work that way. Many were created by a company or a small team. That team often holds a big pile of the coins from day one. They can change the supply, change the rules, or lose interest and move on to the next project. River Learn's lesson points out that insiders in these projects usually know far more than the people buying.

## The cast iron skillet

Think about your kitchen. There's probably a cast iron skillet that's been around forever. It's not exciting. Nobody's running ads for it. It just does the job, year after year.

Then there's the drawer full of gadgets. The avocado slicer. The egg separator. The thing from the late-night commercial. Each one came with a big promise, and most of them were made by somebody who mainly wanted to sell you a gadget.

Bitcoin is the skillet. Most other coins are the gadget drawer. Some gadgets work fine. But if you want something to rely on for decades, you want the plain tool nobody's selling you a story about.

## Questions I get about this

### What's the difference between bitcoin and crypto?

Crypto is the whole category of digital coins, numbering in the thousands. Bitcoin is one coin in that category, the first and largest, with a fixed 21 million limit and no company in charge of it.

### Is bitcoin safer than other cryptocurrencies?

It has some advantages: no company running it, a fixed supply, and the longest track record. That doesn't make it safe. Its price still swings hard and can stay down for years.
