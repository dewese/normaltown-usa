## X / Twitter
Most care has two prices.

The "list price" they run through insurance, and the "cash price" you get by just asking.

The cash one is often way lower.

Magic words at the front desk: "What's the cash price if I don't use insurance?" 👇

## LinkedIn
A small habit that saves real money at the doctor: ask for the cash price.

A lot of care has two prices. A high "list price" built for insurers to negotiate against, and a lower cash price for someone paying on the spot. Billing insurance is slow and costly for providers, so many will happily charge less for cash.

The script: "What's the cash price if I don't use insurance?" or "Is there a self-pay discount?"

One catch: cash usually doesn't count toward your deductible, so ask for both numbers and pick the smaller one. Full how-to in comments.
