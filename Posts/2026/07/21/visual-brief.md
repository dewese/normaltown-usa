# Visual Brief — "The Cash Price Secret"

**Core idea:** Same care, two prices. The cash price is often far lower than the list/insurance price.

**Graphic:** `two-prices.svg` (+ `two-prices.png`) — 1:1, 1200×1200.
- Tall white outlined bar = LIST PRICE. Short cyan bar = CASH PRICE (the point).
- Downward white arrow in the gap = the drop. Manrope labels (white / cyan).
- Reuses the bar motif from premium-dollar for a family look. Soft-black canvas, 3 colors only.

**Alt text:**
A tall bar labeled list price next to a much shorter cyan bar labeled cash price, with a downward arrow showing how much the price drops when you pay cash.

**Placement:** after the "Why two prices even exist" section. PNG only for upload.
