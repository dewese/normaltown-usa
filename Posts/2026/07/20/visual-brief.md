# Visual Brief — "What Is Health Sharing?"

**Core idea:** Many people each chip in a little to cover one person's big bill (the barn-raising analogy).

**Graphic:** `crowd-covers-one.svg` (+ `crowd-covers-one.png` preview) — 1:1, 1200×1200.
- 16 white outlined "member" dots in a ring, each with an arrow flowing inward.
- ONE cyan circle (#2DD4FF) at center = the one member's bill being covered. The cyan is the point.
- Canvas soft-black #0F0F0F, white line art, Manrope (no type used in final — pure geometry). No title, no branding.
- Motif reuse: same circle + arrow primitives as the visual vocabulary / premium-dollar, for a family look.

**Alt text:**
A ring of sixteen people each sending a contribution inward to cover one member's medical bill, showing how health sharing spreads one big cost across a crowd.

**Placement:** in-body, right after the "How it actually works" section (where the mechanics land). Also use as featured/social image. Always upload the PNG, never the SVG.
