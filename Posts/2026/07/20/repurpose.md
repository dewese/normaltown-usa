## LinkedIn
Health sharing, explained plainly:

A group of people agree to cover each other's large medical bills. No insurance company in the middle. You pay a flat monthly fee, handle the first $500 of an event, and the crowd funds the rest.

The upside: their money comes from that one flat fee, so they don't profit from denying you.

The catch: it's not insurance. No legal guarantee, and a poor fit for anyone who needs ironclad coverage.

A fair look at who it fits and who it doesn't. Link in comments.
