## LinkedIn
The most underrated money move isn't an investment. It's a $1,000 cushion.

Here's what happens without one: the water heater dies, the car needs $600, and with no cushion it goes straight onto a credit card. One normal-sized emergency becomes lasting debt.

Why $1,000 and not "six months of expenses"? Because a huge target makes people freeze. A grand is close enough to actually reach, and big enough to catch most surprises.

Name a separate account, automate $25/week, feed it any windfall, then leave it alone. How-to in comments.
