Everything you need to paste this article live on beehiiv. Body text lives in
`article.md` (source of truth); this file has the SEO fields, image placement, the
two small edits, and the publish checklist.

## STEP 0 — one-time setup (you do this; I can't create accounts/logins)
1. Create the beehiiv account + a publication named **Normaltown USA** (free Launch plan).
2. **Connect your custom domain BEFORE publishing anything.** (Publishing on a beehiiv
   subdomain first and moving later throws away early SEO equity.)
3. Verify the site in **Google Search Console** once the domain resolves.
