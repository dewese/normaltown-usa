# Outline — "Nobody Gets Paid to Make You Well"

- **H1:** Nobody Gets Paid to Make You Well
- **Meta:** You pay for health insurance every month and still feel like the system isn't on your side. Here's the simple money reason — and what aligned care could look like.
- **Promise:** The reader will finally understand *why* the system feels stacked against them — and feel empowered, not scared.
- **The one analogy:** The mechanic who's paid by the visit, not by the fixed car. (Stated up front; returns at "who gets paid," at the insurer twist, and at the close.)
- **Money-bucket:** Health→CrowdHealth. **Word target:** ~1,150 (cornerstone).

### Beats
1. **Name the feeling** — you do everything right, still feel the system isn't on your side. (no claim)
2. **Follow the money** — every business makes money from what it sells; ask what health gets paid for.
3. **The mechanic paid by the visit** — the core analogy.
4. **Who gets paid, and for what** — doctors under fee-for-service = paid by the piece. [VERIFY: fee-for-service rewards volume]
5. **"But isn't there a rule?"** — the 80/20 / Medical Loss Ratio rule, explained plainly. [VERIFY: 80/20 rule + 2024 rebates]
6. **The twist** — a capped slice means they win when the *whole bill* grows.
7. **The part nobody says** — nobody's paid when you're simply well; cured = lost customer, managed = subscription.
8. **Not about villains** — frame the plumbing, not the people (guardrail).
9. **What aligned incentives look like** — flat-fee model → honest CrowdHealth turn + disclosure + honest limits. [VERIFY: CrowdHealth $60 flat, not-insurance]
10. **What you can do this week** — ask the cash price; ask "what will this change?"; guard your own health.
11. **Takeaway** — once you see it, surprise bills become a map.
12. **CTA + disclaimers + affiliate disclosure.**
