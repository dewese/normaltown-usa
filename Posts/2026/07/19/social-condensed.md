## LinkedIn
Every business earns from what it sells. So what does the health system actually get paid for?

Doctors, under fee-for-service, are paid per visit and procedure. That's volume, not outcomes.

Insurers can keep about 20 cents per premium dollar, so their profit only grows when your total bill grows.

Follow the logic and you land somewhere uncomfortable: nobody earns a dime on the day you're simply well.

Not a knock on doctors. A look at the wiring underneath them. Link in comments.
