## X / Twitter
You have insurance and STILL got a bill. Not a mistake. It's the design.

Insurance isn't all-you-can-eat. It's:
- a deductible (you pay full price first)
- coinsurance (you split the check after)
- networks (off-menu = full price)

Know these 3 and stop getting blindsided 👇

## LinkedIn
Why you can "have insurance" and still owe real money:

Insurance isn't an all-you-can-eat pass. It's a cost-share with three "you first" rules.

1. Deductible: you pay full price for care until you've spent a set amount. Resets every January.
2. Coinsurance: even after that, you still pay a slice of every bill.
3. Out-of-network: go off the approved list and the discount disappears.

They stack, which is how one visit becomes a shock bill. Not bad luck, just the design. How to play it better, in comments.

## Facebook / Instagram
"I have insurance, why did I get a bill?" You're not imagining it.

Think of an all-you-can-eat buffet that still charges you per plate until you've spent thousands. That's the deductible. Then coinsurance splits the check. Then out-of-network charges full price.

They stack. Know your deductible number and always ask the cash price.
