## LinkedIn
If you're around bitcoin at all, you'll hear a riddle: "not your keys, not your coins." Here's what it actually means.

Picture two ways to have a hundred dollars. One, it's folded in your own pocket. You can spend it any second. Two, a buddy is holding it for you. He says it's yours, but if he loses it or skips town, it's gone. You had a promise, not the cash.

The "key" is a secret code that controls the coins. When you buy on an app and leave it there, the company holds the key. Usually fine, but you're trusting them.

Honest part: holding your own key means full control and full responsibility. Lose it and there's no help desk. Small amount, an app is fine. Bigger amount, worth learning to hold it yourself. In comments.
