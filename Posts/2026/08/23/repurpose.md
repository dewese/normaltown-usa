## LinkedIn
Ever notice the news says inflation is cooling, but the store still costs a fortune? You're not confused, and the news isn't lying. They're measuring different things.

Picture hiking a steep hill. The trail gets less steep. You're still climbing, it's just not getting harder as fast. That's what "inflation coming down" means. Inflation is the speed prices rise, not the prices themselves.

Here's the part nobody explains: slower price increases are not falling prices. The high prices from the last few years are baked in. They stopped rising fast; they didn't reverse.

So budget for where you're standing now, not the prices you remember. Normal moved. Full take in comments.
