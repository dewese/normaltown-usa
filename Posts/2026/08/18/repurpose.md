## LinkedIn
There's a calm way to buy something whose price jumps around, and it removes almost all the stress.

Picture filling a bathtub. You could dump one giant bucket and hope the water's warm. Or let the faucet run a steady trickle so it averages out. Buying a little each week is the trickle. Betting it all at once is the bucket.

The nice part happens on its own: your fixed amount buys more when the price is low and less when it's high, without you ever having to be clever. Nobody reliably times the perfect moment anyway.

Honest catch: a steady trickle protects you from bad timing, not from the thing itself going down. Only use money you won't need soon. Full take in comments.
