## LinkedIn
Most budgets die in the first busy week, and it's not your fault. They're built for someone with a quiet life and a free Sunday. Here's one built for a real week.

Stop building a spreadsheet. Build a grocery list. Three lines:

1. The bills that don't change. Add them up once.
2. The number you're allowed to spend. What's left after bills and savings. This is the only number you watch.
3. The save-first move. Move your savings on payday, before you can spend it.

It survives because it asks almost nothing of you during the week. Automate what you can, watch one number, done. Full version in comments.

## Facebook / Instagram
If your budget always falls apart by Wednesday, the budget is the problem, not you.

Most budgets ask you to track every dollar in every aisle. Nobody has time for that. So build a grocery list instead: your fixed bills, one spending number to watch, and savings you move on payday before you can spend it.

Set it once, automate what you can, watch one number. Full plain-English version at the link in bio.
