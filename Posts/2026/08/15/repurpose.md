## LinkedIn
Here's why a medical bill can still surprise you even though you pay for insurance every month.

A deductible is the part of the bill you cover before the plan pays anything. Picture an umbrella that won't open until the first inch of rain has already landed on your head. That first inch is your deductible. Once you've paid up to that line, the umbrella finally opens.

Quick plain-English glossary while we're here:
- Premium: the monthly bill to have insurance at all.
- Copay: the small flat fee at the visit.
- Out-of-pocket max: the ceiling on your worst year.

One useful move: before you hit your deductible you're paying full price anyway, so it's worth asking for the cash price. Full explainer in comments.
