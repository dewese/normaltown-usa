## LinkedIn
I write about health sharing as a real option, so let me do the honest opposite: who it's wrong for.

A motorcycle is great. Cheap, fun, easy to park. But if your job is hauling lumber every day, it's the wrong tool, no matter how good it is. Health sharing works the same way.

It's wrong for you if:
- You have ongoing medical needs. Sharing is built for the big surprise, not steady known care, and often excludes pre-existing conditions.
- You need a legal guarantee. Sharing is not insurance; there's no contract forcing the group to pay, even though in practice they do.
- The community's rules don't fit your life. Read them; if they don't fit, walk away.

If that's you, it's a no, and a good no. Who it's right for, in comments.
