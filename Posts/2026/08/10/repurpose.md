## LinkedIn
Here's one idea that explains a lot about money: value sticks to things that are hard to make more of.

Picture the front row at a concert. Limited seats, can't print more, so they hold their worth. Now imagine the band could print unlimited front-row seats. Yours would be worth almost nothing.

Regular dollars are closer to "unlimited seats" than most people realize, which is why a dollar buys a little less each year. Some things have a built-in hard cap instead. That's the whole idea behind the scarcity people talk about.
