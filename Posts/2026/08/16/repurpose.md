## LinkedIn
Here's the simplest test for whether money will hold its value: is it hard to make more of?

Picture two neighbors handing you an IOU. The first can print his at home whenever he likes, so each one means less. The second can only make one by doing something slow and costly he can't fake. That IOU holds its value, because making it is hard on purpose.

Gold worked as money for centuries for exactly that reason. It's hard to dig up, so nobody can flood the world with ten times more of it overnight. Easy-to-make money leaks value for the opposite reason.

Honest catch: difficulty is one ingredient in holding value, not the only one. Full plain-English take in comments.
