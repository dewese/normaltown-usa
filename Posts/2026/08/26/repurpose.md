## LinkedIn
Here's something nobody explained to me about "the rich get richer": it's partly just the order of a line.

Picture a buffet. The front of the line fills their plates while prices are low. Partway through, the restaurant raises the prices. By the time you reach the front, you pay more for the same plate.

New money works like that. Whoever gets it first spends it while prices are still low. As it spreads, prices drift up. Regular people are near the back, through wages and everyday spending, so your money arrives after prices have already moved.

It's also why a raise doesn't feel like a raise. You can't jump the line, but you can stop holding everything in cash that shrinks while you wait. Full take in comments.
