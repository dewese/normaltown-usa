## LinkedIn
If you feel like you're running just as hard as ever and staying in the same place, here's one reason that isn't your fault.

Your dollar has a slow leak. Picture a tire with a pinhole. Drive on it a while and you feel nothing. Leave it a month and it's flat. The air slips out a little at a time.

Money does the same, because more of it can always be made. When the pile grows, each dollar you hold buys a little less. That slow slide is what people mean by "inflation."

You can't patch the whole tire yourself. But you don't have to keep every dollar in the spot that leaks fastest. Plain-English version in comments.

## Facebook / Instagram

It's nothing you did. But once you see it, you can stop keeping all your money in the spot where the air escapes fastest. Full plain-English explainer at the link in bio.
