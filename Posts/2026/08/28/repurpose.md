## LinkedIn
If you pay your credit card faithfully and the balance still barely moves, the card is working exactly as designed. Here's the picture.

A carried balance is a boat with a slow leak. Your payment is you bailing with a cup. The interest is the hole letting water back in. Bail about as fast as it leaks, and the level never drops. Card rates are often over 20 percent, so that's a lot of water, and the minimum payment is the smallest cup they'll let you use.

Two moves to drain it:
1. Pay more than the minimum. Every dollar above it hits the balance itself.
2. Attack the highest-rate card first, minimums on the rest, then roll that payment forward.

And build a small cushion so the next surprise doesn't go back on the card. Full version in comments.
