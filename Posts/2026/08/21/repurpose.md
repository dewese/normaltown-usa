## LinkedIn
People ask me if they should wait for the price to drop before they buy. Fair question, and a trap I've watched smart people lose years to.

The best time to plant a tree was twenty years ago. The second best time is today. You can't plant it in the past, and wishing you had grows no shade. The only move you have is to plant one now.

Here's the humbling part: nobody reliably calls the top or the bottom. Not the pros, not the billion-dollar funds. Prices move on surprises, and you can't schedule a surprise. Most people who try to time it just watch from the sidelines while it drifts up without them.

So I don't guess. I buy a small fixed amount on a schedule and let time average it out. Honest catch in comments.
