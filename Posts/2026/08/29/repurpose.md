## LinkedIn
The word "wallet" confuses everyone new to bitcoin, because a bitcoin wallet isn't a pouch holding coins. Here's what it actually is.

Picture a mailbox on a post. It has a slot on top where anyone can drop a letter, and a door on the front that only your key opens. That's a wallet. The slot is your address, which you can share freely so people can pay you. The door is your key, which you guard and never share.

Here's the twist: the coins aren't "in" the wallet at all. They live on a giant shared record the whole network keeps. Your wallet just holds the key that proves which coins are yours. It's a keychain, not a coin purse.

Two kinds: a company holds your key (easy, but you're trusting them) or you hold it yourself (full control, full responsibility). Full explainer in comments.
