## LinkedIn
Most of us are paying to light rooms we never walk into. Subscription rooms.

Picture a big house with every light on, but you only ever sit in two rooms. That's your subscriptions: added one at a time, each small and reasonable, adding up to a mansion you don't live in. They're designed to be forgettable, because a charge you never re-decide is the easiest money a company can collect.

The fix is a one-time, 20-minute job:
1. Pull your last month of statements.
2. Circle everything that repeats.
3. Ask of each: did I use this last month? If no, cancel it today.
4. Move what you saved straight to savings.
