## LinkedIn
People ask if health sharing is smart or too good to be true. Here's the honest comparison, no hype.

Insurance is a catered dinner: you pay the caterer, and they're contractually required to show up with the food. Reliable and regulated, but you're also paying for the company, its profits, and its lawyers.

Health sharing is a neighborhood potluck: everyone brings a dish, and when one family's in trouble, the table fills up. Cheaper and friendlier, with no profit skimmed off the top.

The real trade-off is the guarantee. Insurance is a legal contract. Health sharing is not insurance and doesn't legally guarantee your bill, though the group has every reason to pay. Cheaper and fairer incentives, in exchange for a commitment instead of a contract.

Neither is a scam. Full side-by-side in comments.
