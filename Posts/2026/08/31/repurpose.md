## LinkedIn
"How much should I put in?" is the smart question to ask about anything that swings in price. Here's a plain way to size it, with no magic number.

Think of hot sauce. A few dashes make a good meal better. Empty the bottle and dinner's ruined. Your savings, emergency fund, and bills are the meal. A risky, swingy investment is the sauce. A dash adds flavor; drowning the plate is how people get hurt.

The one rule that actually protects you: only put in money that, if it went to zero tomorrow, wouldn't change your life. Not rent, not the emergency fund, not tuition. If losing it would mean a missed bill, it's too much.

And get the meal on the table first: emergency fund, high-interest debt, bills. Then a small dash makes sense. Full take in comments.
