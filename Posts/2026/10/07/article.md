# One Heart Procedure, Three Prices: $83,655, $44,000, and $22,000

> A member needed a heart rhythm procedure. His local hospital quoted $83,655. A hospital 90 miles away quoted $44,000. One a few states away quoted $22,000. With travel, the whole thing came to about $30,000. He paid $500, and 150 members funded the other $29,500 in three days.

If I told you a gallon of milk cost $8 at one store and $2 at another, you'd drive. This is that, with a heart.

## What happened?

CrowdHealth published this couple's story back in 2022. He's self-employed. His wife had lost her job, and the family's insurance went with it. They'd joined CrowdHealth a few months earlier and weren't sure yet that a crowd of strangers would really show up.

Then one day, mid-workout, his heart rate shot up to about 230 beats a minute and wouldn't come back down. The ER figured out what it was: a fast heart rhythm problem. The fix is a procedure where a doctor goes in and corrects the spot causing the trouble.

It worked. He was cleared with no restrictions afterward.

## What did the bill look like?

This is the part that made my jaw drop. Same procedure, three hospitals:

- **Local hospital:** $83,655
- **A big-city hospital about 90 miles away:** $44,000
- **A hospital a few states away:** $22,000

That's not a typo. The far one was about a quarter of the local one.

So they went. Plane tickets, three hotel nights, a driver, meals, and the procedure came to about $30,000 all in. That was more than $50,000 under the local quote, even after paying for the trip.

He paid his $500. CrowdHealth asked 150 members to fund the remaining $29,500. The money was there in three days, before the procedure.

## What did CrowdHealth actually do?

They did the shopping. They took the quotes, called other hospitals, and found the $22,000 one. They booked the travel and lined up a local person to drive the couple around once they landed. Then they sent the request to the crowd.

This is the part I love. I've never heard of an insurance company calling three hospitals to find a member a better price. Why would they? It isn't their job.

## What's the honest part?

He had to get on a plane for a heart procedure. That's a real ask. Some folks can't travel, or don't want to be far from home for something scary. In a true emergency, you don't get this choice at all. This worked because he was stable enough to plan.

The couple also said they were nervous the whole time. They hadn't seen the crowd come through yet. I think that's normal, and I'd rather tell you than pretend it feels like a sure thing.

Two more notes. This happened in 2022, and CrowdHealth's pricing has changed since then. And the written story and the video description don't match on a couple of small details, like whether 150 or 152 members chipped in. I used the written version.

CrowdHealth says being asked to travel for a big procedure is rare. Its founder put it at around 10 times in 60,000 bills, mostly for folks in very small towns.

## The takeaway

Same procedure, $83,655 or $22,000, depending on the building. Prices for the same care can be four times apart, and most of us never find out because nobody shows us the menu. Having someone make those calls is worth a lot.

## Questions I get about this

### Will CrowdHealth make me travel for surgery?

Usually not. CrowdHealth says travel requests are rare, roughly 10 in 60,000 bills. But funding is based on a fair price for your area, so if local quotes come in far above that, you may be asked to look farther away or cover the gap. That trade-off is real, and I cover it in [what if the crowd doesn't fund your bill](/p/what-if-the-crowd-doesnt-fund-your-bill/).

### Why do hospital prices vary so much?

Because prices aren't posted and most patients never ask. Each hospital sets its own sticker price, and the people paying usually aren't the people choosing. I dug into this in [why cash prices beat insured prices](/p/why-cash-prices-beat-insured-prices/).

### How fast does the crowd fund a bill?

In this story, three days. Across everything, CrowdHealth's August 2026 results showed an average of 7.72 days from a complete bill to money in the member's account. Big, messy hospital bills can take a lot longer to negotiate first.
