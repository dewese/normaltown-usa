# The Fine Print That Catches New Members

> A new dad joined CrowdHealth with a baby on the way, thinking the baby would be covered at birth. A newborn isn't active until two weeks after birth when the pregnancy wasn't eligible. Other new members were surprised that funding requests are approved automatically unless you decline within 48 hours, and that the $99 intro price is per member.

Most of the one-star and two-star reviews I read weren't about a medical bill going unpaid. They were about somebody being surprised. So here are the surprises, so you aren't.

## What happened?

A new father wrote a two-star review this summer. He signed up with a baby already on the way. He says the sales rep told him more than once that coverage starts at sign-up.

Then he learned the baby wouldn't be active until two weeks after birth. Within days of joining he was charged for the membership and had automatically chipped in toward another member's birth. He asked for that money back and was told no.

CrowdHealth replied publicly. They quoted their own emails to him, which said there's a two-week waiting period before a newborn becomes active. They said he had chosen the auto-approve setting. And they explained that the money he chipped in went straight to another member, so they couldn't return it.

I can't tell you who's right about the phone calls. I can tell you the rules.

## What are the rules that surprise people?

**1. Newborns and timing.** If the pregnancy was eligible, meaning a due date at least 300 days after joining, the baby can be added within 30 days of birth. If it wasn't, pregnancy costs aren't shared and the baby can't join until two weeks old.

**2. Requests are approved unless you say no.** When CrowdHealth asks you to help with another member's bill, you get 48 hours to decline. If you do nothing, it counts as a yes. Several members have complained about this in CrowdHealth's own complaints log.

**3. Money you chip in is gone.** It goes directly to another member. It isn't held by CrowdHealth, so there's nothing to refund.

**4. The intro price is per member.** The $99 a month for three months is per person. Two people is $198. Three is $297. CrowdHealth's log shows one new member who expected $35 total and cancelled when it wasn't.

**5. The monthly cost moves.** One member was upset to be charged $250 to $400 in a month. Another said it made budgeting hard. The amount changes each month up to a ceiling. For a family of four, that ceiling is $660 all in.

**6. Declining to help others can hurt you.** CrowdHealth tracks how often you fund requests. Fall below 90% and your own bills can be flagged, which makes them less likely to be fully funded.

**7. If you leave, you can't come right back.** Rejoining requires a 180-day wait.

**8. Not everyone can join.** No one 65 or older. No tobacco users. There are weight limits: 220 pounds for women and 260 for men.

**9. Some states still expect insurance.** CrowdHealth's FAQ says it doesn't satisfy the insurance requirement in California, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Rhode Island, Vermont, or Washington, D.C. Check your own state before you rely on that list.

**10. The fee goes up in January.** The monthly fee rises from $60 to $65 per member on January 1, 2027. For a family of four, that moves the worst-case month from $660 to $680, if the ceiling on the crowd's ask stays where it is.

## Why does it work this way?

Most of these rules protect the crowd from people gaming it.

It's like a neighborhood tool-lending shed. It only works if people can't show up the day they need a ladder, borrow it, and never come back. So there are rules about when you can borrow and what you have to put in. The rules are fair. They're also annoying if you didn't read the sign on the door.

## Questions I get about this

### When is a newborn covered on CrowdHealth?

If the pregnancy was eligible, add the baby within 30 days of birth, and newborn care is its own $500 event. If the pregnancy wasn't eligible, the baby can't join until two weeks old. Check the timing with your advocate well before the due date.

### Can I turn off automatic approval of funding requests?

Members get 48 hours to decline each request, and silence counts as approval. Review your settings when you join. Keep in mind that funding fewer than 90% of requests lowers your score and can affect your own bills. More in [how members vote to fund bills](/p/how-members-vote-to-fund-bills/).

### Is the $99 intro price for the whole family?

No. It's $99 per member per month for the first three months. Two people is $198 a month, and three is $297. After that you pay the regular monthly cost, which I break down in [the real monthly cost of health sharing](/p/the-real-monthly-cost-of-health-sharing/).
