# Sick on a Sunday: A Doctor in 2 Minutes and a $6 Prescription

> A CrowdHealth member woke up sick on a Sunday, opened the app, and had a prescription within 2 minutes and 22 seconds. The medicine cost $6. He felt better within a day. Virtual doctor visits are included on the standard membership, which makes the small, annoying illnesses the easiest part of the whole thing.

Most of what goes wrong with our health isn't a helicopter ride. It's a sore throat on a weekend when everything's closed.

## What happened?

A member woke up feeling lousy on a Sunday. He didn't want to sit in an urgent care waiting room. So he opened the CrowdHealth app and tapped the option to talk to a doctor.

He timed it. Two minutes and 22 seconds later, he had a prescription sent to his local pharmacy.

The medicine cost him $6. He was back to normal within 24 hours.

That's the whole story. I like it because it's so small.

## Are there other stories like it?

Plenty.

A newer member did their first virtual visit this summer. The prescription went to the pharmacy right after. They got a little confused turning in the bill, used the help tool in the app to sort it out, and the money was deposited quickly.

A mom of three wrote on her blog that one of her kids had a stomach bug. They did a virtual visit, got a prescription for the nausea, and the visit cost was refunded within a week.

Another member has used virtual care for travel medicine, a checkup, and a few small infections that needed antibiotics.

## What does the bill look like?

On the standard membership, virtual primary care and urgent care visits are unlimited and can be turned in to the crowd. Starting January 1, 2027, the visit can be with any licensed provider, and up to $129 per visit can be shared without paying your $500 first.

The prescription is where a second lesson hides. CrowdHealth posted a short video where a mom tells this story. A young man with insurance needed a skin medicine. It took about three weeks to get it approved. Then at the counter it was $400. She called her own pharmacy, said she'd be paying cash, and asked the price. It was $26.

Same medicine. $400 with insurance and $26 without.

It's like the price on a concert ticket versus the price after the fees. Somehow the "help" made it cost more.

## What did CrowdHealth actually do?

Put a doctor in the app and a discount card in the member's pocket. That's it. No negotiating, no crowd, no forms.

## What's the honest part?

A video visit can't do everything. A doctor on a screen can't listen to your lungs or look in your kid's ear. One member wanted blood work, and the virtual doctor wouldn't write the order, so they needed a second appointment. CrowdHealth's own complaints log from this August mentions a member whose virtual visit didn't meet their expectations.

The prescription rules are tight too. CrowdHealth doesn't share brand-name drugs when a generic exists. Ongoing medications are limited to a 12-month supply. And prescriptions have to be bought with CrowdHealth's discount tool to count. If you take an expensive medicine every month for the long haul, read [health sharing and prescriptions](/p/health-sharing-and-prescriptions/) before anything else.

Also, the cheaper membership option with the $15,000 commitment doesn't include funded virtual visits. This applies to the standard one.

One more caution. That first review is from 2022, and the service may work differently now. I can't promise you 2 minutes and 22 seconds.

## The takeaway

For the everyday stuff, I don't need a crowd. I need a doctor I can reach on a Sunday and a pharmacy price that isn't a joke. A quick visit and a $6 prescription is the kind of boring win that adds up over a year.

## Questions I get about this

### Does CrowdHealth include telehealth?

Yes, on the standard membership. Virtual primary care and urgent care visits are unlimited, and members report prescriptions sent to their local pharmacy right after the visit. From January 2027, up to $129 per visit can be shared without meeting the $500 first. The lower-cost membership with the $15,000 commitment doesn't include funded virtual visits.

### How do I find the cash price for a prescription?

Call the pharmacy, say you're paying cash, and ask. Then compare a discount card price. In one story CrowdHealth shared, a medicine was $400 through insurance and $26 cash. More ways to do this in [the cash price secret](/p/the-cash-price-secret/).

### Does CrowdHealth pay for prescriptions?

Some. Prescriptions tied to an eligible health event can be shared if you use CrowdHealth's discount tool. Brand-name drugs with a generic available, and maintenance drugs past a 12-month supply, aren't shared.
