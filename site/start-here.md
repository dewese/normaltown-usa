# Start Here

Hey, welcome. Glad you found this place.

Normaltown USA is a plain-English guide to money and health for regular people. No jargon, no scare tactics, no ten-dollar words where a one-dollar word will do. Just how this stuff actually works, the way I'd explain it to a friend across the kitchen table.

## Who this is for

You work hard. You pay your bills. You pay for health insurance every single month. And money and healthcare still feel rigged against you, like everybody got a handbook except you.

You don't need a finance degree, and you don't need more guilt. You just want someone to explain the game in normal words so you can play it a little better. That's the whole job here.

## Who I am

I'm David. I spent 25 years in web design and marketing, and 25 years making music and touring. My wife and I have two little girls, and somewhere along the way I got tired of being confused by my own money and my own medical bills. So I did the homework, and I write down what I find. More on the [About page](/about/).
