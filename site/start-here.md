# Start Here

Hey, welcome. I'm glad you found this place.

Normaltown USA is a plain-English guide to money and health for regular people. No jargon, no scare tactics, no ten-dollar words where a one-dollar word will do. Just how this stuff actually works, explained the way I'd explain it to a friend across the kitchen table.

## Who this is for

You work hard. You pay your bills. And still, money and healthcare somehow feel rigged against you, like everyone got a handbook except you. You don't need a finance degree or more guilt. You just want someone to explain the game in normal words so you can play it a little better.

That's the whole job here.

## What I write about

Three things, all in plain English:

- **Money** for normal people. Why you feel broke, where your money leaks out, and small moves that actually help.
- **Health, and the bills.** Why the system works the way it does, and how to stop overpaying for it.
- **Saving in something that lasts.** A calm, honest look at bitcoin and what "holding value" even means.

## My promise to you

I tell you the downside too. If something has a catch, I'll say so, right in the same breath. No bait-and-switch, ever. I'd rather lose the sale than lose your trust. (And when I ever earn a little from a link, I'll tell you plainly.)

One more thing: I'm not a financial advisor. I'm just a guy who did the homework and takes good notes. You make your own calls.
