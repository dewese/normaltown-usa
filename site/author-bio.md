# About

Howdy, I'm David Dewese. I've spent the last twenty-five years working in web design and marketing while also making music and touring (twelve albums, shows on five continents). After starting a family, I got interested in finding ways for us to thrive, not just survive, on an artist's income.

I've always been underwhelmed by how money and healthcare get explained to regular people. It's buried in jargon and seems built to confuse. So I started Normaltown USA to share what I've learned in plain English, the way I'd tell a friend. No hype, no bait-and-switch, just what I've actually figured out.

I'm not a financial advisor, just a guy who's doing the homework.

## What you'll find here

Three things, one idea per post, short enough to read with your coffee.

- [Money](/money/): why you feel broke on a decent income, where it leaks out, and small moves that actually help.
- [Health](/health/): why medical bills surprise you, how to pay less for care, and an honest look at health sharing.
- [Bitcoin](/bitcoin/): why the dollar loses value and where bitcoin fits as savings, not gambling.

If you're new, the [Start Here](/start-here/) page has the three posts I'd read first.

## How I do the homework

I use the stuff I write about. My family of four left regular health insurance for a health sharing service called CrowdHealth, and I write about what we actually do, not what a brochure says. Before I publish a number, I check it against the source, usually the company's own published guidelines or the government's own figures, and I note when it was last checked. When something has a catch, I say so in the same breath as the upside. If I get something wrong, I fix it, and the post shows the date it was updated.
