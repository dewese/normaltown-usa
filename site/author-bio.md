# About

Howdy, I'm David. I've spent the last twenty-five years working in web design while also making music and touring (twelve albums, shows on five continents). After starting a family, I got interested in finding ways for us to thrive, not just survive, on an artist's income.

I've always been underwhelmed by how money and healthcare get explained to regular people. It's buried in jargon and seems built to confuse. So I started Normaltown USA to share what I've learned in plain English, the way I'd tell a friend. No hype, no bait-and-switch, just what I've actually figured out.

I'm not a financial advisor, just a guy who's doing the homework.
