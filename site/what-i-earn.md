# What I Earn

Most sites that recommend things to you will not tell you what they get paid. I think that's backwards. If money is changing hands because of something I wrote, you should be able to see the number before you decide whether to trust me.

So here it is, in dollars.

## The short version

Two links on this site pay me. Everything else is free, and always will be.

| Who | What you get | What I get |
|---|---|---|
| CrowdHealth, code **NORMAL** | Your first 3 months at $99 a month | $250, once you've been a member 3 months |
| River (bitcoin) | Up to $100 in bitcoin, same as me | Up to $100 in bitcoin, same as you |

Neither one costs you a penny extra. The CrowdHealth discount is money in your pocket that you would not get by going straight to their site.

## The CrowdHealth one, in detail

My family of four has used CrowdHealth since 2022. I wrote about it for a long time before there was any referral money in it, and I'd still write about it if the program went away tomorrow.

Here's exactly how it works:

- You join through my link with code **NORMAL**.
- Your first 3 months cost $99 a month instead of the normal rate.
- If you're still a member in good standing after 3 months, CrowdHealth pays me **$250** by bank transfer at the end of the following month.
- If you sign up and cancel in month two, I get nothing. That's the right way around, and I want you to know it works that way.

**What that means for you, honestly:** I have a financial reason to want you to join and stay. I'm telling you that up front because it's true, and because a site that pretends otherwise is lying to you about something small in a way that should make you doubt the big things.

**What I do about it:** I write the parts that cost me money. [Who health sharing is wrong for](/p/who-health-sharing-is-wrong-for/) talks people out of it. [What CrowdHealth doesn't cover](/p/what-crowdhealth-doesnt-cover/) is a list of reasons to say no. [What if the crowd doesn't fund your bill](/p/what-if-the-crowd-doesnt-fund-your-bill/) is the scary one, and I wrote it anyway. If I only wrote the good parts, the $250 would be worth more to me than you are, and it isn't.

## The River one, in detail

River is where I buy bitcoin. The referral is split evenly, which is unusual and is part of why I like it.

- We each get $5 when you buy your first $100.
- We each get another $15 at $1,000.
- We each get another $80 at $10,000.
- It caps at $100 per person.
- It's not offered in every state.

Same amount on both sides, every step. I'm not getting a cut of you. We're getting the same thing.

## What I turn down

I get emails asking me to write about insurance brokers, supplement companies, credit repair outfits, and a steady stream of crypto projects. I say no to all of it. The rule is simple: if my family doesn't use it, it doesn't go on this site, no matter what the offer is.

I've also said no to paid placement on the [Resources](/resources/) page. The two things on it are there because we use them, not because they bid highest.

## What I don't do

- I don't get paid per click. Only if you actually join and stay.
- I don't get paid more for writing something nicer. The payout is the same either way.
- I don't have a contract that tells me what I can or can't say. Nobody reviews my posts before they go up.
- I'm not employed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with CrowdHealth or River. I'm a customer with a link.
- I don't sell your email address or your data to anybody, ever.
