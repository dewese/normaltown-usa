## CrowdHealth facts gathered 2026-10-28 (from joincrowdhealth.com)

- **Monthly cost history (average ask + $60 fee, before 1% to 3% card/ACH fee).**
  Under 55, 2026: Jan $125, Feb $145, Mar $155, Apr $140, May $135, Jun $135, Jul $135,
  Aug $140, Sep $155, Oct $165. 2025: Oct $140, Nov $150, Dec $145. 2024: Nov $160, Dec $165.
  55-64, 2026: Jan $190 ... Oct $270. Family of 4, 2026: Jan $435 ... Oct $555 (2025 Nov $505,
  Dec $490). Pattern: the ask rises in late year and has dropped back each January. Range 2026
  under 55: $125 to $165.
- **Crowdfunding maximums (from /fold):** Original: $140 per member per month (0-54), $280
  (55-64), $420 (families of 4+). Black Swan: $40, $80, $120.
- **Fold+ Bitcoin Savings Program (/fold):** link a Fold+ account; each month CrowdHealth
  sweeps the difference between your crowdfunding maximum and what you actually contributed
  into your Fold+ account. CrowdHealth covers the Fold+ subscription while you're a member.
  (Fold is a separate bitcoin company; David uses River for his own buys. Say so.)
- **Black Swan:** $15,000 member commitment per health event; only bills over $15,000 go to
  the crowd. Not available with Black Swan: annual wellness, maternity, unlimited virtual care,
  virtual talk therapy, quarterly specials. Monthly fee: don't state it; send readers to the
  pricing calculator (/pricing lets you pick $500 or $15,000).
- **Member guide:** bills must be submitted within 6 months of the date of service; members
  have two days to approve or deny each crowdfunding request; self-pay discounts 25% to 85%;
  no doctor networks; tell CrowdHealth about a health event before scheduled or expensive care.
  Not eligible: tobacco users (daily for a year or more, ever), men over 260 lbs, women over
  220 lbs (exemptions in CA and NY).
- **Member tools:** dedicated Care Advocate (same person each time); bill negotiation
  ("70%, 80%, even over 90% off" is CrowdHealth's claim, attribute it); Find a Doc;
  MedalistRx prescription discount card in the app; annual wellness up to $300 with the $500
  waived; maternity $3,000 commitment; unlimited virtual care via HealthTap; talk therapy via
  BetterHelp (moves under the $300 wellness in 2027); quarterly specials (past: CAC score, gut
  health, DEXA, fasting insulin, VO2 max), announced at year end.
- **Open enrollment (healthcare.gov, read 2026-10-28):** Nov 1 start; Dec 15 last day for a
  Jan 1 start; Jan 15 end (Feb 1 start). State exchanges can differ.
