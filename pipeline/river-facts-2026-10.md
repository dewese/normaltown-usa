# River facts sheet (read from river.com and support.river.com, 2026-10-28)

Use these for November 2026 posts. Anything with a number gets "as of October 2026".
River changes products often; recheck before reusing in a later month.

## The sign-up CTA (end of every bitcoin post)
Link: `https://river.com/signup?r=EYL3QNCO`. Referral terms (river.com/rewards, unchanged
2026-10-28): both sides earn the same, paid in bitcoin. $5 each when the new person buys $100,
$15 more each at $1,000, $80 more each at $10,000. Up to $100 each. Not available in every state.

## What River is
- US company, **bitcoin only** (no other coins). A brokerage: you buy from River at a quoted
  price; it's not an order-book exchange.
- App Store rating shown on site: 4.9.
- Financials page: profitable, zero debt, $68.1M corporate treasury (end of 2025); as of Q3 2026,
  $7.2B lifetime trading volume and $2.8B bitcoin under custody. Annual audit by an independent
  accounting firm; 2025 statements downloadable.

## Fees
- One-time buys and sells: 1.00% up to $1M (lower tiers above). Minimum fee $0.01.
- Spread is included in quoted price: typically about 0.25% on buys, 0.50% on sells.
- **Recurring buys: 0% fee** after the first 7 days. Weekly/biweekly/monthly: the first order
  pays the normal fee, every order after is free. Daily/hourly: free from day 8.
- Direct deposit auto-conversion to bitcoin: free up to $30,000 per account per month.
- Custody: free. One free on-chain send (withdrawal) per month; otherwise network fees apply
  (network fees go to miners, not River).
- Instant cash withdrawals: 1% (min $1, max $150). Standard cash withdrawals free.

## Recurring buys
- Frequencies: hourly, daily, weekly, every two weeks, 1st and 15th, monthly.
- Pay from linked bank or River cash balance. Daily/hourly debits the bank once a week
  (a $10 daily buy pulls $70 a week).
- Runs until you cancel. You can edit the amount and payment method; changing the schedule
  means cancel and restart.

## Supercharged recurring buys
- Optional add-on: if the price at your scheduled time is at least about 1% below a moving
  average, you buy more automatically. Multipliers: 25%, 50%, 100%, or 200%. Example: $10 buy
  with 100% supercharge buys $20 on a dip.
- Moving average: 7 days for hourly/daily/weekly; 14 days for biweekly and 1st & 15th;
  30 days for monthly. No extra fee.
- Honest note: it spends more cash on dips, so your total spend varies. Need the cash there.

## Target Price Orders
- Target Buy / Target Sell: set a price, it executes automatically if reached. Minimum $10.
- The cash (or bitcoin) is reserved until it fills or you cancel. Normal one-time fee applies.
- Honest note: price may never reach your target; that's guessing, and recurring buys don't
  depend on guessing.

## Bitcoin Interest on Cash
- 3.50% APY as of 10/03/2026, **variable**, paid in bitcoin (or dollars if you choose).
  Interest accrues daily, converts to bitcoin daily, paid out monthly.
- Cash is held at Lead Bank (Member FDIC), pass-through FDIC insurance up to $250,000.
  FDIC covers Lead Bank failure, not River's failure, theft or fraud. Bitcoin is not FDIC
  insured.
- Not a yield product: River does not lend your bitcoin. Cash deposits take about 7 days to
  settle; withdraw settled cash anytime, arrives within 2 business days.
- River compares to national checking average (0.07% per FRED, Oct 2025) and says most big
  banks pay about 0.10%.
- Interest is generally taxable income (say "talk to a tax pro").

## Banking with bitcoin
- Direct deposit your paycheck to River; choose how much auto-converts to bitcoin and how much
  stays in cash earning interest. Paid up to two days early. Free conversion up to $30k/mo.
- Pay bills, rent, credit cards with cash or bitcoin; River account and routing number.
- No debit card, credit card or checks (per River's FAQ).
- Selling bitcoin to pay a bill may be a taxable event.

## Proof of Reserves
- Monthly. River publishes total assets (bitcoin in its cold storage, visible on a block
  explorer like mempool.space) and total liabilities (anonymized list of client balances).
  You can check your own balance is on the list with your account ID.
- Proves control by sending a small transaction with a predetermined amount.
- Oct 6, 2026 proof: assets 33,404.73 BTC, liabilities 33,332.42 BTC, reserve ratio >100%.
  Monthly history back to Nov 2025 all >100%.
- River says it does not lend out client bitcoin. Limitation (River Learn says this too): a
  proof is a snapshot in time and doesn't show every debt a company has; that's why the
  financials page matters too.

## Security
- 100% of client bitcoin in multisig cold storage (offline). SOC II compliant. 2-factor
  authentication, new device verification, account notifications, 24/7 monitoring.
- **ForceField:** a weekly limit on how much bitcoin can leave your account. Turning it off
  takes 5 days and River notifies you daily. Raising the limit also takes 5 days; lowering is
  instant. Doesn't affect buying, selling, receiving, or adding/withdrawing cash. While on, you
  can't create or edit auto-withdrawals (existing ones keep running). Protects against stolen
  phone, stolen password, scams and phishing.
- River: "Most bitcoin theft happens through scams, not hacks." River never asks for sensitive
  info via social media or messaging apps.

## Send, receive, Lightning
- Send/receive on-chain or over Lightning. Three on-chain speeds: priority, standard, free
  (one free send per month, resets on the 1st).
- **River Link:** send or gift bitcoin by sharing a link in any messaging app; no address or
  invoice needed. Can be printed as a PDF with a QR code for a physical gift. The recipient
  claims it to River or another wallet. Sender's account must be verified.

## Auto-withdrawals
- Automatically send bitcoin to your own wallet address once your settled balance reaches a
  threshold you pick (minimum 0.005 BTC). Optionally leave some on River.
- David uses this to move his weekly buys to his own cold storage hardware wallet.

## Beneficiary plan
- Name people or trusts as beneficiaries; creates a transfer-on-death (TOD) registration.
  One plan per personal account; not for business accounts. Edit in the app.
