# Gallbladder Surgery, Funded Before the First Cut

> One CrowdHealth member went to the ER, learned their gallbladder had to come out, and had the surgery funded before it was even performed. Another member's gallbladder surgery took longer to settle than CrowdHealth expected, but it was paid. Gallbladder removals often top $10,000 in CrowdHealth's results and get spread across dozens or hundreds of members.

The gallbladder is a small organ that helps you digest fat. When it acts up, it usually has to come out. It's one of the most common surgeries there is, which makes it a good everyday test.

## What happened?

**Funded ahead of time.** A member landed in the ER and found out they needed their gallbladder removed. CrowdHealth referred them to a surgeon whose price the member thought was reasonable. Then the crowd funded the surgery before it was done. The member said CrowdHealth kept them posted the whole way.

**The slow one.** Another member described their gallbladder surgery as costly. Getting it all settled took longer than CrowdHealth had told them to expect. But in the end, the member said, they came through.

**The busy year.** A couple who joined in early 2025 wrote that in a short stretch they'd had a pregnancy, an emergency surgery, and gallbladder problems. Their short version: bills get paid back quickly.

## What do the bills look like?

None of those three shared totals. So here's what CrowdHealth's public results show.

One request for an ER visit with gallstones and surgery was more than $10,000 and went out to 244 members. Another gallbladder removal over $10,000 went to 54.

Now for a number from outside CrowdHealth that I think about a lot. A national magazine profiled a member who, years before joining, needed gallbladder surgery while he had no coverage at all. He'd been told it would cost $34,000 through insurance. He paid cash and got it for less than a quarter of that.

Same surgery. He just asked what it cost for someone paying directly.

That's like finding out the "list price" on a mattress is $3,000 and the store sells it for $700 every single weekend. The list price was never the price.

## What did CrowdHealth actually do?

In the first story, two things that matter. They pointed the member to a fairly priced surgeon, and they got the money together before the surgery date.

Funding first is a big deal. It means you aren't putting a five-figure surgery on a credit card and hoping.

## What's the honest part?

Timing isn't promised. One member was funded before surgery. Another waited longer than they were told. If you need a date you can count on, you won't get one in writing.

Emergencies also don't leave room to shop. If your gallbladder attack lands you in the ER at 2 in the morning and they operate that night, nobody is comparing surgeons. The negotiating happens afterward, on a hospital bill. That can be slow, as I covered in [the collections post](/p/when-the-hospital-wont-negotiate/).

The first member had the best version: a diagnosis in the ER, then enough time to pick a surgeon and plan it.

And gallbladder trouble often shows up as stomach pain for a while before anyone names it. If you had those symptoms and saw a doctor about them in the five years before joining, that could count as pre-existing. The rule is about symptoms, not only a diagnosis.

## The takeaway

A common surgery, a fairly priced surgeon, and money in hand before the operation. That's the best this model has to offer. The speed isn't guaranteed. The lesson about list prices is free: ask what it costs when you're paying directly, because the first number is rarely the real one.

## Questions I get about this

### How much does gallbladder surgery cost without insurance?

It depends on where it's done and whether it's an emergency. CrowdHealth's results show gallbladder requests above $10,000. One man in a national magazine profile was quoted $34,000 through insurance and paid less than a quarter of that in cash. Always ask for the direct-pay price.

### Can CrowdHealth pay for surgery before it happens?

Yes, for planned procedures. One member's gallbladder removal was funded before the surgery. You'll need a written estimate from the surgeon, and you should open the event with your advocate as early as you can.

### What if my surgery is an emergency?

Get the care. The bill gets negotiated afterward, and you pay the first $500 of the event. Emergency hospital bills take longer to settle than planned surgeries. My steps are in [health sharing and the ER](/p/health-sharing-and-the-er/).
